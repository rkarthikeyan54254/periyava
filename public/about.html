Ask Mahaperiyava

A respectful way to explore Sri Mahaperiyava’s documented teachings without asking a language model to stand in for the source.

Beta

This is an independent research beta, not an official Sri Kanchi Kamakoti Peetham website. Answers may take a few seconds because the service retrieves and checks supporting evidence before presenting a response.

What is Pramāṇa?

Pramāṇa is an evidence-first devotional research project. Its purpose is to make important Hindu source traditions easier to explore while preserving a clear distinction between documented source evidence, editorial curation and generated explanatory prose.

What sources does this site currently use?

The current Mahaperiyava experience is grounded in the seven verified volumes of Deivathin Kural, also known in English as Voice of God. The service searches curated teaching records derived from those volumes and returns source labels with supported or qualified answers.

If those seven volumes do not establish a sufficiently direct answer, the site should say so. That is a statement about the current verified corpus—not a claim that Sri Mahaperiyava never spoke on the subject elsewhere.

How is an answer produced?

  1. Your question is normalized for a small set of obvious spelling mistakes and interpreted in English or Tamil.
  2. The Pramāṇa retrieval layer searches the verified teaching records for relevant evidence.
  3. An evidence-sufficiency gate decides whether the material supports a direct answer, only a qualified answer, or no responsible attribution.
  4. When synthesis is used, a language model may help phrase the answer naturally. The model is not treated as the source.
  5. Supporting teachings and evidence boundaries remain available with the answer.

What should I expect?

  • Ask naturally. English, Tamil and common Roman-Tamil phrasing are supported.
  • Questions about practice, dharma, prayer, education, family life, emotions and texts are welcome when asked respectfully.
  • The site may qualify an answer when the corpus supports a related principle but not the exact personal instruction requested.
  • The site is not an oracle, a substitute for a guru or ācārya, or a tool for personal religious rulings.
  • Generated wording is not presented as Sri Mahaperiyava’s verbatim speech unless it is explicitly marked as a quotation.

Why only one follow-up?

The follow-up feature is intentionally bounded. It lets you refine one answer once, but the follow-up is independently re-grounded in the source evidence. There is no open-ended conversation memory. This keeps the product closer to an evidence-guided research experience than a chatbot.

Why is there no “questions answered” counter?

Pramāṇa optimizes for evidence quality rather than engagement volume. A public question counter can reward quantity without saying anything about correctness. The more meaningful number today is source coverage: seven verified volumes, with answer boundaries shown explicitly.
